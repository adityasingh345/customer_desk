## Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to Rs 3,000.

## Do you provide GST invoices?
Yes. A GST invoice is emailed automatically after every order. For an older order, email hello@greenleaf.example with your order number.

## How long do refunds take?
Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days to the original payment method.
