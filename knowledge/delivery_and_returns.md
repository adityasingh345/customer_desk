# Green Leaf Organics - Delivery and Returns

<!-- SAMPLE DATA for the demo. -->

## Do you deliver?
Yes, we deliver within Pune city. Orders above Rs 500 get free delivery. For orders below Rs 500 the delivery fee is Rs 40.

## Do you deliver outside Pune?
No, we do not deliver outside Pune at the moment.

## How long does delivery take?
Orders placed before 2:00 PM are delivered the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM are delivered the next morning between 8 AM and 11 AM.

## What is your return policy?
Fresh produce can be returned or replaced within 24 hours if it is damaged or not fresh. Packaged items can be returned within 7 days if they are unopened and you have the bill.
